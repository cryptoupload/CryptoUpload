Why this exists
----------------

App Store terms of service (device attestation, DRM interoperability
requirements, resale/redistribution restrictions, etc.) can be read as
conflicting with rights GPLv3 guarantees to downstream recipients. Without
this exception, publishing an official CryptoUpload build on an App Store
could be read as a breach of GPLv3 by the store's own terms, even though the
corresponding source remains fully available under GPLv3. This exception
resolves that conflict for App Store distribution specifically, without
weakening copyleft anywhere else: modified versions must still be shared
under GPLv3, and the source of what's on the App Store must still be public.
